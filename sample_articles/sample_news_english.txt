City Council Approves New Bus Lane Pilot
By Jordan Lee
2026-05-21

The city council approved a six-month bus lane pilot on Thursday, according to the transportation department's public report. The report states that the pilot will cover 8.5 kilometers across three corridors and cost about $4.2 million. Department officials said the goal is to reduce average bus delays by 12 percent during peak hours. A neighborhood business association said it supports faster buses but requested monthly parking impact data. The council said the first public performance dashboard will be released in August 2026. More details are available at https://example.gov/bus-pilot.
